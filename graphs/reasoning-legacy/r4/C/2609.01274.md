## Limitations and conditions

- "This is not a claim that the RL model literally executes any external decoding policy; we make no parameter-level
  mechanistic identification." (L393)
- "paired Base/RL checkpoints from a single RL recipe (SimpleRL-Zoo)" (L409); "Training-duration effects at the scale of
  prolonged RL training [Liu et al., 2025] remain untested" (L409) — the "internalised search" reading is confined to
  this recipe and duration
- "The predictor still depends on a cohort of RL models rather than a single target, and its behavior beyond the tested
  cohort is not established." (L405)
- "the number of regimes (3) is determined by the four available benchmarks and the minimal AIME sample size" (L228); the
  floor regime is statistically indistinguishable from the maths regime (L228, Table 19)
- "In our experiments, $\mathcal{B}=\{1,2,4,8,16\}$ ." (L70) — budgets up to 16; beyond it the error rises (L234)
- "Recoverability statements should be interpreted relative to the UDF policies actually evaluated in each cell" (L397)
- "Cross-benchmark numbers should be read with this regime structure in mind, not as a uniform metric." (L401)
- "the four unrecoverable cells are all (model, Math500) where RL has discovered a sharper distribution than any base
  policy at any budget" (L713) — in self-consistency, RL does something base search does not
- "GPQA comparisons that cross sampling batches therefore carry a $\pm$ 3–4 pp caliber" (L824)
- "the rule’s role is path selection rather than match discovery" (L222) — each RL target already has several Base
  matches within ±3 pp, so recovery is easy to find; structure is the claim
- "five individual cells exceed it" (L365) — on held-out benchmarks, five cells exceed the 8 pp criterion (weak, cross-family
  or maths-specialised models)
