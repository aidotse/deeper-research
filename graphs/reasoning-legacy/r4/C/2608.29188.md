  analysis to Countdown." (L249)
- "Our PPO run uses rollout group size $G{=}1$ , no actor KL penalty, and a 1,024-token training rollout cap" (L249) —
  and one seed ("with seed 1", L934)
- "the second run is a public GRPO series we evaluate rather than train" (L249)
- "On standard math benchmarks the first complete calculation is an observable proxy for an entrance, not a solver-defined
  one." (L249)
- "The supplied-entrance quantity $E_{t}^{\mathrm{do}}$ is interventional" (L249) — it is not the policy's own execution
  term
- "the decomposition assigns 34.5% of the change in solve probability to access reallocation and 65.5% to conditional
  execution" (L549) — most of the accuracy gain is execution; the access finding is about breadth
- "long-horizon execution acts as a compounding secondary bottleneck" (L207) — the entrance account weakens on long
  reasoning
- "a broader set of 276 zero-access family cells spanning 250 problems achieves mean supplied-entrance completion 0.087"
  (L555) — deep-tail families reopen far less than the 0.212 headline
