## Limitations and conditions

- "We deliberately study reasoning cores of fewer than 1M parameters, isolating the recurrence mechanisms from
  large-scale pretraining confounds" (L393) — and "Whether identity-biased gating and LayerScale still suffice as $d$ ,
  $d_{\mathrm{ff}}$ , and $L$ grow by orders of magnitude remains open." (L393)
- "Our three interfaces (topological masking, RoPE, standard attention) were hand-selected per task" (L397) — the
  structure each task gets is hand-designed
- "we treat the recurrence step count $T$ as an external hyperparameter" (L401) — no learned halting; ACT halting fails
  to extrapolate (Table 5)
- "but collapses abruptly at 10 hops, indicating a generalization boundary enforced by the topological masking" (L267) —
  graph extrapolation reaches 1.6x the training depth, then fails; capacity dependent
- "It, nonetheless, approaches chance by depth 9." (L295) — unstructured-text extrapolation is weak (60% OOD mean)
- "leaves ID and mean OOD accuracy essentially unchanged but collapses training stability" (L323) — the ablation without
  LayerScale and gate matches or beats the full model on mean accuracy (97 vs 95 nested, 62 vs 60 family); the recipe's
  benefit is seed stability
- "We compare our silent-thinking objective against an intermediate-supervision baseline on the graph reachability task"
  (L327), at "$d=256$ because with smaller capacity ( $d=128$ ), neither objective propagates past $8$ hops" (L333) — the
  supervision finding is shown on one task, at a widened model
- "measured on GPT-2 medium (Radford et al., 2019) ( $N{=}24$ )" (L385) — the memory comparison sets the core's footprint
  against another model's KV cache; it is a cost model, not a matched-accuracy comparison
