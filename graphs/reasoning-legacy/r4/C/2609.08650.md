## Cells

- `method name`: confirmed — DATPO — "we propose DATPO (Difficulty-Adaptive Sentence-entropy-guided Tree-structured Policy
  Optimization)" (L11)
- `method summary`: corrected — tree-structured rollouts at train time whose branching budget scales with the prompt's
  failure rate (1 − mean reward of N base rollouts), forking at the starts of the highest-entropy sentences; the policy is
  updated per block of the tree with Monte Carlo value differences as advantages, plus an annealed sibling-diversity bonus
  (embedding distance, positive-advantage blocks only). It changes the objective as well as the rollout structure. —
  "we scale the tree expansion proportionally to this difficulty" (L125); "Advantages are computed and assigned at this
  block level." (L137); "we apply this bonus exclusively to blocks with positive base advantages" (L149)
- `datasets / models`: corrected — trained on MATH (12,000 problems); evaluated on MATH500, AIME26, AIME25, AIME24, AMC23,
  and out of domain on GPQA-Diamond and a 500-item MMLU-Pro subset; Qwen2.5-3B-Base and Qwen3-4B-Base; baselines GRPO
  (with clip-higher and token-level loss), Dr.GRPO, TreeRL, AttnRL — "We use Qwen2.5-3B-Base (Yang et al., 2024) and
  Qwen3-4B-Base (Yang et al., 2025) as base models." (L203)
- `metric`: corrected — avg@k is the primary metric; pass@k for coverage; maj@k for test-time scaling (k = 8 on MATH500,

## Limitations and conditions

- "calculating the sibling-diversity term requires additional forward passes through an external embedding model, which
  introduces computational overhead" (L303)
- "Deriving both the empirical difficulty and Monte Carlo state values from a limited number of rollouts (e.g., $N=4,B=4$
  ) can inject noise into the estimations" (L305)
- "resource limitations restricted our empirical validation to the 3B-4B parameter regime" (L307) — 7B and above
  unverified
- "as our evaluation primarily focuses on mathematical reasoning" (L307) — other domains untested, apart from the small
  out-of-domain check (GPQA, MMLU-Pro)
- "tree-based methods generally incur a higher wall-clock time compared to standard parallel rollouts" (L971) — 60.3 h vs
  26.3 h for GRPO at comparable tokens (Table 9)
- "For robustness, the pass@ $k$ results are computed by averaging over three independent evaluation runs." (L207) — the
  repeats are of evaluation, not of training; no repeated training runs are reported
- "to compare the methods applied to Qwen2.5-3B-Base" (L233) — the test-time-scaling (maj@k) claim rests on one model
- "while these exact behavioral patterns may not identically manifest in every setup" (L525) — the difficulty-regime
  account is interpretive; the Section 2 analysis used a different setup (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, GRPO-Zero without KL or
  clipping, 1024-token generations; L549, L573)
- "we set the sampling temperature to 1.0 and the maximum generation length to 1024 tokens" (L941) — short training
  generations

## Other findings

- The strongest baseline differs by model: on Qwen3-4B, plain GRPO (58.3 pass@k) beats AttnRL (57.4), so the gain over
  the best baseline there is +2.1, not the +3.0 over AttnRL that the text reports.
- DATPO branches wider than the baselines (B = 4 vs 2); Appendix F.6 tests the baselines at the same width and finds no
  gain for them.
