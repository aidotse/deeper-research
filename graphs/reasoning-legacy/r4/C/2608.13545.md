## Limitations and conditions

- "we acknowledge that certain emergent behaviors like in-context learning may be less pronounced than at frontier
  scales" (L230) — the ICL null may be a scale effect
- "Crucially, within the tested post-training budgets, we observe no difference between LittleLearner post-trained on K–5
  data versus Beyond-K–5 data." (L180); "for the 5B LittleLearner and the few shot and explanation setups we test" (L194);
  "sets the effective capability ceiling in our tested settings" (L204) — the null results are bounded by the budgets
  and setups tried
- "post-training only modestly improves LittleLearner while producing considerably larger gains for Unfiltered" (L180) —
  post-training does help a little Beyond-K–5
- "This trend partly reflects the limitations of grade-level CCSS labels as a proxy for problem difficulty for language
  models." (L156) — the grade split is an imperfect difficulty axis; Grade 6 overlaps K–5 (L442, L466)
- "it should not be interpreted as a model of a human child" (L222) — skills do not follow the human curriculum's order
  (L444)
- "removed passages are systematically longer and lexically more complex than retained ones" (L102) — the filter keeps
  about 35% of K–5 text and skews toward simpler prose, so the corpus differs from Unfiltered in style as well as scope
- "The filter retains 2.48% of 6,000 Beyond-K–5 passages" (L104) — leakage is small but not zero
- "Beyond-K–5 MathCAMPS pass@1 increases for both LittleLearner (1.1% $\rightarrow$ 1.8%) and Unfiltered (4.6%
  $\rightarrow$ 10.3%)" (L392) — tokenisation affects the arithmetic gap, which persists
