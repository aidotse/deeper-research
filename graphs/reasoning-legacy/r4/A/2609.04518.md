## Limitations and conditions

- "Every estimate other than the Within and Cross held-out levels rests on one checkpoint, and the source harnesses were evaluated at two attempts per task." (9 Limitations, line 149) — single-checkpoint and low-attempt estimates.
- "Absolute solve rates are low ( $1$ – $10\%$ ) and the endpoint is binary." (9 Limitations, line 149) — floor regime; the null may not carry over to stronger policies.
- "Training covers one model family (Qwen Team, 2025b; Hui et al., 2024), one budget, four source harnesses, one round of on-policy re-collection, and Python-only tasks." (9 Limitations, line 149) — scope.
- "The design resolves differences of about $1\,\mathrm{pp}$ and larger." (9 Limitations, line 149) — the null result means no effect at or above about 1 pp, not no effect.
- "The six held-out contrasts were fixed before the checkpoints existed and not registered externally" (9 Limitations, line 149) — pre-specification was not external.
- "The second-model comparison uses one untrained checkpoint per model, under context budgets that differ between the two families" (9 Limitations, line 149) — the model-dependence of harness strength rests on a thin comparison.
- "Multilingual, test-generation and contamination-hardened variants of the benchmark" (9 Limitations, line 149) — these lie outside the study.
- "Each is a separate intervention from the one isolated here." (Scope of the result, line 137) — the null speaks only to the grouping boundary with exposure fixed. It does not cover larger task pools, longer training, or harness co-evolution.
- "The four arms train for a single epoch of offline replay over one frozen manifest with precomputed advantages" (Appendix G, line 370) — offline, single-epoch regime; only one arm is partly on-policy.
- "As assembled, it removed 9% of that offset." (6, line 121) — the residualized arm failed to remove the harness offset, so it does not test offset removal.

## Other findings

- The paper frames its result as a portability null: RL on this corpus yields configuration adaptation, not portable capability, under either rule. On the held-out harness, even the SFT warm start does not separate from the untrained base. The draft does not convey this, and it matters for any reading of the cluster `The harness as a scaling axis`.
