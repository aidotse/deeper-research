## Cells

- `method name`: corrected — three-mode self-routing GRPO (NoThink / Short / Long) — "We presented three-mode self-routing for GRPO, in which a reasoning model chooses whether to answer quickly, reason briefly, or reason at length by emitting a single routing token." (Discussion and Conclusion)
- `method summary`: corrected (mechanisms added) — The model emits a routing token (NoThink, Short or Long) as the first token of its response, trained end-to-end inside GRPO with no separate router. Four pieces keep routing from collapsing. (1) A per-mode discounted reward (bases 1.3 / 1.2 / 1.0) makes each mode reward-optimal in its own length band, with crossovers near 800 and 3,000 tokens. (2) Hard per-mode caps (1,024 tokens for NoThink, 3,000 for Short, Long uncapped) score any over-cap answer as wrong. (3) A balance term toward a 1/3 share is added to the advantage after mean-centring, with std-normalisation disabled. (4) A forced-rollout warmup bootstraps emission of the routing token. Validation generation is uncapped. — "Our method overcomes collapse with two mechanisms working together. First, hard per-mode token caps make the modes distinct by construction" (Introduction); "Default caps are 1,024 tokens for NoThink and 3,000 for Short; Long is uncapped." (Method, Three Modes with Hard Token Caps); "We use GRPO mean-centering across the group but disable standard-deviation normalization" (Method, Advantage Estimation)
- `datasets / models`: corrected — Training on MATH-lighteval. In-distribution evaluation on MATH-500 (temperature 0.6). Out-of-distribution evaluation on GSM8K (avg@5) and AIME 2024 and 2025 (avg@16). Model: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B; GRPO with G=8 for 90 steps, three seeds. Baselines: three single-mode ablations (NoThink-, Short-, Long-only) and the untrained base. A preliminary negative result uses Countdown. — "For out-of-distribution evaluation we use GSM8K (Cobbe et al. 2021) (1,319 grade-school word problems, avg@5) and AIME 2024 and 2025 (30 competition problems each, avg@16)." (Experimental Setup); "We use DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B (Guo et al. 2025), a 1.5B-parameter reasoning model distilled from DeepSeek-R1" (Experimental Setup)

- `result`: corrected (numbers confirmed, comparisons and conditions added) — On MATH-500, accuracy is 0.782 versus 0.796 for the base, and mean length falls from 4,796 to 2,811 tokens (41%). ## Limitations and conditions

- "The results cover three seeds of one 1.5B model on one mathematical training distribution." (Discussion and Conclusion, Limitations) — scope of every result.
- "The mode caps are task-specific and must be matched to the target task’s length distribution" (Limitations) — the method needs per-task tuning of caps.
- "uncapped validation makes brief-mode accuracy marginally optimistic relative to the trained objective." (Limitations) — evaluation condition that flatters the brief modes.
- "Accuracy does not rise above the base model: it dips while routing is being established and recovers to $0.783$ by step 90" (Token Efficiency) — the gain is token efficiency only; accuracy is traded down.
- "On AIME, where nearly all problems require extended reasoning, the router correctly declines to cut budget" (Token Efficiency) — savings depend on a heterogeneous difficulty mix; there is little saving on hard sets.
- "This failed, for a reason in the task rather than the constants. For routing to be learnable the modes must differ in value on some problems" (Appendix A) — a precondition for the method: on Countdown, with uniform difficulty, the router collapsed.
- "Empirically the binary router collapsed within 30–50 steps in every configuration" (Appendix A) — the negative result behind the cap design.
- "On a small distilled model the reward alone cannot enforce this choice" (Discussion and Conclusion) — the result depends on the hard caps, not on reward shaping alone.
- "realised length is a proxy for the routing split, which the released logs do not record per OOD benchmark." (Figure 10 caption) — the claim that the router sends GSM8K to NoThink and AIME to Long out of distribution is inferred from length, not measured routing.
- "$\beta_{\text{bal}}$ is annealed to $0$ once routing is established, to verify the split is calibrated to difficulty rather than held at $1/3$ by the term." (Appendix B, Balance term) — the routing split was obtained with a balance term toward equal shares, removed later.

## Other findings

- The paper's numbers for the base model are not internally consistent. Table 2 gives step-0 accuracy as 0.807 "under the routing prompt"; Table 5 gives 0.796. The abstract's base length is 4,796, against 4,743 in Table 5. The abstract figures (0.782 vs 0.796) match Table 5.
- On GSM8K the router loses about 5 accuracy points against the base (0.781 vs 0.831). The draft's phrase "at higher accuracy than baselines of similar length" is true only against the brief fixed modes. The paper's statement that "The router stays within one to two points of the base" (Appendix D) does not hold for GSM8K in its own Table 5.
- The AIME token reduction is 12% in the main text and 13% in Table 6.
