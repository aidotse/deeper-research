## Limitations and conditions

- "Future work includes extending this analysis to high-dimensional generative models where behaviors correspond to richer semantic modes rather than mixture models." (4 Conclusion, line 764) — every result is in a toy mixture model, and the transfer to LLMs is not shown.
- "We use a shared covariance $\Sigma$ (with bounded spectrum) for both modes so that separation and overlap are controlled purely by the means and the mixture weights." (2.1, line 167) — the core theorems assume equal-covariance Gaussians. The appendices extend them to log-concave families and K modes.
- "toward a true target $p_{\alpha}=\alpha p_{\mathrm{o}}+(1-\alpha)p_{\mathrm{n}}$ that explicitly preserves the old behavior" (2.5, line 421) — the reverse-KL "no mass forgetting" result rests on the RL target already containing the old mode with weight α. It holds by construction of the target.
- "Assume the old mean is already correct: $m_{\mathrm{o}}=\mu_{\mathrm{o}}$ (with $m_{\mathrm{n}}$ arbitrary)." (Theorem 2.3, line 311) — the drift bound is a gradient bound at the correct old mean, not a bound on trajectory drift.
- "Theorem 2.4 says that once the reverse-KL dynamics enter a neighborhood in which the local curvature stays uniformly positive" (2.3.1, line 367) — exponential convergence is local, inside a certified basin.
- "In practice, however, purely on-policy stochastic optimization can suffer a finite-batch starvation failure when $\beta$ becomes small" (2.5, line 421) — the population-level reverse-KL retention can fail in finite batches without replay.
- "numerator replay provides the minimal rehearsal mechanism by which SFT can avoid strong forgetting in this toy setting" (2.5, line 419) — the authors themselves call it a toy setting.
- "retention requires the anchor to be sufficiently strong, and the threshold (3.14) makes this tradeoff explicit" (3.2, line 672) — the TTT-Discover retention condition.
- "OAPL cannot entirely discard the old mode unless the frozen reference has already done so" (3.3, line 756) — OAPL's retention depends on the reference policy.
- "It avoids mass forgetting if the demonstrator is strong enough" (1, line 99) — the SDFT retention condition.

## Other findings

- `evidence` should be `proven`, not `derived`. The paper states and proves theorems; their generality is limited by the toy model, not by approximation.
- The paper reports no experiments at all. The "empirical observations" mentioned are Chen et al. (2025)'s, which motivated the mixture model.
- The SFT = forward-KL and RL = reverse-KL mapping is a modelling choice made in Appendix A. The claims about real SFT and RL inherit this abstraction.
