## Limitations and conditions

- "Our recoverability and rule results are behavioral statements about the operating-point landscape" (Limitations, line 393) — the paper does not claim the RL model performs search internally, and makes no parameter-level identification.
- "Some settings expose AR-only pools while others include expanded controllers" (Limitations, line 397) — recoverability holds only relative to the policies actually evaluated in each cell.
- "GPQA often behaves as a sharpening/concentration task rather than a support-expansion task" (Limitations, line 401) — metrics differ in meaning across benchmarks, and IFEval is treated as an OOD stress test.
- "The predictor still depends on a cohort of RL models rather than a single target, and its behavior beyond the tested cohort is not established." (Model-specific parameters, Limitations) — the base-only offset needs an RL cohort.
- "Results cover one Qwen-family scale sweep (0.5B/1.5B/7B/14B), one same Hard RL-training split cross-family check (Qwen2.5-Math-7B), one cross-family check (Llama-3.1-8B), and one weak-capacity check (Mistral-7B-v0.1)" (Limitations, line 409) — coverage is narrow, from a single RL recipe.
- "Training-duration effects at the scale of prolonged RL training [Liu et al., 2025] remain untested" (Limitations, line 409) — the internalised-search reading is untested for long RL training.
- "The label is descriptive rather than a capability claim" (6.2, line 228) — the AIME floor regime is not statistically distinguishable from the math regime. The number of regimes is set by the four benchmarks available.
- "whereas H1 is at parity ( $-0.04$ pp, $[-2.69,+2.50]$ )" (6.2, line 234) — BOPTR does not beat base greedy decoding on the anchor model.
- "Extending the budget grid to $b=64$ , and to $b=256$ on AIME, raises the mean error to 5.02 pp, concentrated on AIME at 12.50 pp" (6.2, line 234) — the headline holds for budgets up to 16.
- "although five individual cells exceed it" (6.3, line 333) — held-out transfer fails in weak-capacity, cross-family and math-specialised cells.
- "Cross-family degradation delimits this interpretation" (Conclusion, line 383) — direct anchor-path transfer degrades across model families.
- "When recovery fails, or when SC exposes a ceiling, the gap points to behavior not captured by the tested policy pool" (7, line 377) — some of the RL gain is not recovered.

## Other findings

- The "qualified internalized-search" reading means RL improves sampling efficiency toward points Base+search can reach. It holds under the SimpleRL-Zoo recipe and short training, and the authors explicitly decline to make it a general predictor.
- The 3.07 ± 0.39 pp three-seed replication varies decoding seeds on a fixed policy pool. It is not a replication across pools or recipes.
