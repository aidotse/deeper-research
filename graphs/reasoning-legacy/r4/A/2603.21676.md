## Cells

- `method name`: corrected — depth-recurrent Transformer (`vertical Chain-of-Thought`), built from a task-invariant reasoning core trained with a silent-thinking objective — "We study vertical Chain-of-Thought: adding test-time reasoning compute by iterating a shared-weight block in latent space rather than generating tokens" (6 Conclusion, line 435)
- `method summary`: corrected — A single shared-weight Pre-LN Transformer block (the `invariant reasoning core`) is applied T times to a hidden state, with a learned depth embedding per step. A hand-chosen, task-specific perception interface (adjacency mask, RoPE, or plain attention) encodes the input, and a task-specific readout decodes the final state. Stability rests on supervising only the final output (silent thinking), LayerScale (used on the two sequence tasks only, omitted on graph), and an identity-biased gate (bias −2.0). Models are small (<1M parameters), trained from scratch per task, with T sampled from a bounded training range and set externally at inference (no learned halting). — "For the topological interface, where the hard adjacency mask strongly constrains the computation, we omit LayerScale" (Architecture of f_θ, line 140); "We deliberately study reasoning cores of fewer than 1M parameters" (Scale, line 393)

## Limitations and conditions

- "We deliberately study reasoning cores of fewer than 1M parameters, isolating the recurrence mechanisms from large-scale pretraining confounds" (Scale, line 393) — toy scale.
- "Whether identity-biased gating and LayerScale still suffice as $d$ , $d_{\mathrm{ff}}$ , and $L$ grow by orders of magnitude remains open." (Scale, line 393) — the stability recipe is untested at scale.
- "Our three interfaces (topological masking, RoPE, standard attention) were hand-selected per task" (Manually Designed Perception Interfaces, line 397) — results depend on hand-designed, task-specific priors.
- "At inference time, we treat the recurrence step count $T$ as an external hyperparameter" (Fixed, Externally Specified Depth, line 401) — the right depth is given, not learned; the learned-halting (ACT) variant fails OOD.
- "the learned halting budget is calibrated to the training distribution and does not extrapolate" (Fixed, Externally Specified Depth, line 401) — adaptive depth is unsolved.
- "This boundary is capacity-dependent: a wider model propagates several hops further" (4.1, line 267) — the graph extrapolation limit depends on width.
- "Graph reachability (adjacency masking) is precise but brittle" (5.1, line 355) — near-perfect graph extrapolation holds only to 1.6× and then collapses.
- "stronger inductive biases yield precise in-distribution behavior but brittle extrapolation, while weaker biases sacrifice precision for robustness" (5.1, line 357) — trade-off conditioning all three results.
- "It is also the one task where a fixed-depth Transformer, even with several times more parameters, extrapolates only one hop before falling to chance" (5.1, line 355) — the clear advantage over fixed depth is on one task; on the sequence tasks the models are within a couple of points.
- "We deliberately widen the dimension to $d=256$ because with smaller capacity ( $d=128$ ), neither objective propagates past $8$ hops." (4.5, line 331) — the intermediate-supervision result is measured at widened capacity on the graph task only.
- "correspondingly small when reasoning is short relative to the prompt" (5.4, line 381) — the memory saving over token CoT shrinks when reasoning is short relative to the prompt.

## Other findings

- There is no accuracy comparison against token-level (horizontal) CoT. The memory and latency advantage is an analytical cost model plus a KV-cache measurement on GPT-2 medium, not a head-to-head reasoning comparison.
- The draft says the three ingredients `keep the recurrence stable`; the paper's own ablation shows they do not change mean accuracy, only seed-to-seed variance. This qualifies the stabilisation claim.
- Out-of-range depth embeddings are untrained; the authors test this confound (zero, clamp, re-init) and find accuracy differs by at most 0.5 points (5.2).
- The draft's `result` carries a process note about `snippet_search`; it should be dropped now that the full text is read.
