# Map of 2609.04518: What Does Multi-Harness RL Learn? Credit Assignment and Portability in Coding Agents

## Method
- L14-16 (Multi-Harness RL Question): "The phrase multi-harness RL does not specify how those harnesses interact during credit assignment." and "Exposure does not determine the grouping rule." — isolates grouping rule from exposure
- L18-22 (Controlled Experiment): "We isolate that boundary in repository-level coding. Four production harnesses collect a frozen trajectory corpus on a shared SWE-Gym pool. From the same Qwen3-8B supervised warm start, the two primary RL arms replay identical records, tokens, rewards, loss masks, and update budget." — controlled experiment with frozen data
- L18-19 (GRPO Variants): "Within forms GRPO groups inside each task–harness pair. Cross pools all harnesses for the task." — two credit assignment rules under comparison
- L82 (Evaluation Method): "The primary crossed campaign evaluates six checkpoints on four source harnesses over 500 SWE-bench Verified tasks at two attempts per task, or 24,000 attempted episodes" — sealed oracle evaluation on held-out harness
- L62-67 (Formal Method): "Write an episode as $e=(x,h,j)$ with task $x$, harness $h$ and rollout index $j$, with sealed-oracle reward $r_{x,h,i}\in\{0,1\}$" and "A^{Within} = (r - μ_h) / σ_h" versus "A^{Cross} = (r - μ) / σ" — formalized advantage computation

## Setup
- L18 (Data Source): "Four production harnesses collect a frozen trajectory corpus on a shared SWE-Gym pool" — uses SWE-Gym benchmark
- L18 (Model and Initialization): "From the same Qwen3-8B supervised warm start" — base model is Qwen3-8B
- L82 (Training Setup): "All four arms replay the identical 81,216 -record manifest (5,543 episodes over 183 tasks) and realize the same 81,200 logged optimizer steps" — standardized training across arms
- L82 (Evaluation Setup): "It evaluates every resulting checkpoint with one sealed per-instance oracle on the four source harnesses and on a minimal harness absent from training." — sealed oracle evaluation
- L85 (Harness Details): "Aider, OpenHands, Qwen Code, and SWE-agent" — four production harnesses in study
- L106-107 (Re-Collection): "Re-collecting the data on-policy does not change this. A further Cross arm spends the same 81,216 updates from the same warm start, replays the frozen records for the first half only, and then re-runs the training tasks" — tests on-policy re-collection variant

## Headline results
- L28 (Harness Dominance): "The evaluation harness moves solve rate by a factor of 4.3 where the training recipe moves it by 1.16" — harness is dominant factor over training recipe
- L85 (Harness Range): "Harness means run from 2.14% to 9.27% over the same 24,000 sealed evaluations" — 7.13 percentage point span across harnesses
- L85 (Recipe Range): "the six recipe means sit between 5.55% and 6.46%" — only 0.91 percentage point span across recipes
- L94 (Held-Out Results): "On the interface excluded from training, the six recipes span 4.01% to 4.73% (Figure 3a, Table 2)." — narrow range on held-out harness
- L94 (Cross-Within Difference): "At eight attempts per task, Cross minus Within is +0.25 pp [-0.48, +1.02]" — no significant difference at held-out harness
- L98 (Source Harness Gain): "On the four training harnesses, Cross is +0.77 pp above SFT, with non-negative point estimates in all four columns" — modest gains on source harnesses
- L113 (Harness Identity in Advantage): "Cross's carries +4.48 pp [+3.22, +5.83]" — pooling carries harness identity signal

## Limitations
- L149 (Model Scope): "Training covers one model family (Qwen Team, 2025b; Hui et al., 2024), one budget, four source harnesses, one round of on-policy re-collection" — single model and budget
- L149 (Task Scope): "and Python-only tasks" — limited to Python code
- L149 (Second Model Comparison): "The second-model comparison uses one untrained checkpoint per model, under context budgets that differ between the two families" — limited to untrained baseline for second model
- L149 (Benchmark Size): "The design resolves differences of about 1 pp and larger." — detection floor of ~1 percentage point
- L149 (Generalizability): "Multilingual, test-generation and contamination-hardened variants of the benchmark lie outside the study." — does not cover all benchmark variants
- L149 (Preregistration): "The six held-out contrasts were fixed before the checkpoints existed and not registered externally" — not externally preregistered despite being frozen before checkpoint creation

## Assumptions and conditions
- L22 (Exposure Fixed): "The evaluation harness, not the training recipe, is the largest effect in the study. Letting harnesses compete for credit changes which trajectories carry gradient and leaves everything downstream of that in place" — assumes harness effects are separable from credit assignment
- L68-70 (Group Composition Assumption): "Both rules are group-relative in the sense of GRPO: the advantage is a standardized reward within a group of sampled trajectories." and "That standardization is not neutral." — assumes group definition affects learning
- L90 (Harness Exchangeability): "Under Cross, then, rewards from contexts that are not exchangeable in the usual same-prompt GRPO sense compete for credit." — assumes Cross violates exchangeability assumption
- L133 (Adaptation vs Generalization): "Configuration adaptation without portable capability: gains of the kind a deployment on the source configurations can use" — distinguishes between adaptation and true capability transfer
- L124 (Action Distribution Assumption): "The policy's actions show the same pattern from the behavioural side." — assumes action distribution reflects learned capability

## Dimensions
- **evidence** — L11 (empirical): "We isolate that boundary in repository-level coding" and "From one Qwen3-8B supervised warm start we replay the same frozen task–harness records from four harnesses" — empirical controlled experiment with frozen data
- **measures** — L28 (capability): "The evaluation harness moves solve rate by a factor of 4.3 where the training recipe moves it by 1.16" — measures solve-rate capability under different harnesses
- **elicitation** — L18 (observational/spontaneous): "Four production harnesses collect a frozen trajectory corpus" — uses natural rollouts from existing systems
- **pressure** — L82 (training-incidental): "replay identical records, tokens, rewards, loss masks, and update budget" — applies standard GRPO training pressure without targeted robustness testing
