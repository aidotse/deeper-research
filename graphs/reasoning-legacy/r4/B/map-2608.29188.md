## Method

- L8-10 (Abstract): "we investigate where inside a reasoning trajectory this breadth is lost: does the policy fail to access a valid solution family, or does it fail to execute computation once initiated? To disentangle access from execution, we analyze the Countdown task, whose solution space can be exhaustively enumerated into discrete entrance families defined by the first operand and operator" — introduces the framework decomposing access from execution.
- L63-74 (Section 3, Equations 1-2): "Given n independent model rollouts Y_{1:n}, the per-instance solution coverage Cov(x,Y_{1:n}) and corpus-level mean coverage Cov(X) are defined as: Cov(x,Y_{1:n})=|{s∈S(x) | s is canonicalized solution}|/|S(x)|... π_θ(solve via b|x)=π_θ(B=b|x)·π_θ(valid completion|x,B=b)" — mathematical formalization of coverage and entrance families.
- L74-75 (Section 3): "We directly measure conditional execution capability E_t^do(b|x) by clamping generation to a solver-constructed entrance prefix e_b" — method for measuring execution independent of access.
- L379-383 (Section 5.4): "peak memory does not change with T at inference, so we take it as the unit... The dashed curves show the key–value cache the backbone builds up while generating T thinking tokens... Our recurrence applies a single block T times, so its critical path is Θ(T)" — memory and latency analysis comparing approaches.

## Setup

- L20-21 (Section 1): "We evaluate this framework across two distinct RLVR implementations: a PPO training trajectory on Qwen2.5-3B and an open-source GRPO checkpoint series on Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct" — two training regimes evaluated.
- L60-61 (Section 3): "For any feasible instance x, an exact symbolic solver generates the complete set of valid arithmetic expressions, canonicalized modulo commutativity and associativity for + and ×. We formalize this search space as a decision tree where depth-one branches represent opening arithmetic decisions and terminal leaves constitute the set of canonical valid solutions S(x)" — task formulation and solution space structure.
- L81-83 (Section 3): "For Countdown, PPO evaluations are conducted using temperature T=0.7, top-p=0.9, a maximum generation budget of 256 tokens, and 320 independent rollouts across 150 held-out solver-feasible problems... GRPO evaluations use identical decoding hyperparameters (T=0.7, top-p=0.9) with a 1,024-token cap, 320 rollouts, and 135 filtered instances" — decoding and evaluation protocol.
- L202-203 (Section 7): "Evaluating Qwen2.5 base and SimpleRL pairs across six standard math benchmarks demonstrates that RLVR consistently triggers early distributional collapse at scale (Table 25; Appendix G.1). While SimpleRL increases macro pass@1 by 13% at 7B and 17% at 14B" — scaling experiments on multiple benchmarks.

## Headline results

- L84-87 (Section 4): "In our self-trained PPO pipeline on Qwen2.5-3B, pass@1 increases more than fiftyfold between steps 50 and 275, while cumulative solution coverage Cov(X) at n=320 samples collapses by 67%, falling from 0.337 to 0.111... From step 25 to step 450, single-sample accuracy more than triples while solution coverage drops by 43%" — main accuracy-breadth tradeoff.
- L130-131 (Section 5): "Under PPO, the entrance segment experiences a substantial likelihood drop of 4.50 nats per token, compared to a negligible shift of only 0.28 nats per token across the remaining reasoning steps, i.e., a 16× disparity in divergence... On 232 valid continuations under GRPO (steps 25 vs. 450), the entrance shift is 8.08 nats per token vs. 0.71 nats, i.e., an 11× ratio" — quantification of where probability mass shifts.
- L132-133 (Section 5): "At PPO step 275, supplying a minimal entrance boosts designated-family completion by more than an order of magnitude, i.e., from 0.018 under free sampling to 0.212... The late GRPO checkpoint exhibits an identical recovery (0.104→0.188)" — recovery with supplied entrance prefixes.
- L162 (Section 5): "for 28 entrance families observed at step 50 but never sampled at step 275 across 320 free rollouts, clamping the minimal entrance achieves a 0.529 designated completion rate at step 275 (higher than the 0.475 baseline at step 50)" — execution capability preserved even for extinct pathways.
- L185 (Section 6, Table 4): "Layer interpolation 0.305 [pass@1], +0.041 [0.020,0.069] [solution coverage]... Checkpoint sampling 0.161 [pass@1], +0.100 [0.059,0.147]" — intervention results for recovery.
- L202-203 (Section 7): "first-calculation entropy drops by 30% at 7B and 41% at 14B, while the distinct-trace rate nearly halves" — generalization to larger models.

## Limitations

- L248 (Appendix A): "Exhaustive enumeration is what makes access and execution separately measurable, which currently ties the direct analysis to Countdown" — entrance decomposition limited to enumerable task.
- L248 (Appendix A): "Our PPO run uses rollout group size G=1, no actor KL penalty, and a 1,024-token training rollout cap" — specific PPO hyperparameters may not generalize.
- L248 (Appendix A): "On standard math benchmarks the first complete calculation is an observable proxy for an entrance, not a solver-defined one" — proxy for entrance on benchmarks without exact enumeration.
- L390-392 (Section 6): "At step 275, 128 problems have complete outputs in both conditions" — limited to problems with complete outputs for some analyses.

## Assumptions and conditions

- L76-77 (Section 3): "Each prefix terminates immediately after the opening operator, enforcing the family constraint while leaving all downstream degrees of freedom entirely unguided. Control baselines, including neutral problem restatements and model-generated failed prefixes, verify baseline completion" — entrance prefix construction and controls.
- L78-79 (Section 3): "Because rarely sampled entrances receive diminishing gradient signals, initial sampling asymmetries compound over training, driving a self-reinforcing contraction of the policy's opening support" — mechanism of gradient-based narrowing.
- L493 (Section 5.3): "we assume the most suspicious 1% of commits will be reviewed by expert humans in a deployment scenario" — audit rate assumption.
- L690 (Appendix E.1): "Starting from successful step-275 samples, the forced-operator arm identifies an alternative first operator that can open at least one valid expression under the same first number, without supplying an arithmetic state" — constraints on forced-operator intervention.

## Dimensions

- L14-16 (Section 1, Abstract): "while RLVR substantially increases single-sample accuracy, recent studies show that post-RLVR policies collapse into narrow answer supports and exhibit severely degraded reasoning diversity... which sharply curtails the marginal gains of repeated sampling" — evidence dimension: empirical measurement of capability collapse despite improved single-sample accuracy.
- L27 (Section 1, Abstract): "demonstrating that alternative solutions remain executable but are no longer initiated. Guided by this localization, we find that while surface prompting fails to recover diversity, entrance-targeted interventions succeed" — measures of execution vs. access capabilities and intervention effectiveness.
- L115-131 (Section 5): "By combining teacher-forced log-likelihood phase attribution with entrance-clamped rollouts, we isolate where probability mass shifts during training. Per-token log-likelihood divergence is 16× (PPO) and 11× (GRPO) larger prior to the first arithmetic operation than across all subsequent reasoning steps" — specific quantification of pressure/reward signal concentration.
- L51-52 (Section 2): "Prior work shows that autoregressive generation is governed by sparse, high-entropy forking tokens that disproportionately dictate downstream trajectory semantics" — relates to how models elicit different solution paths through initial decisions.
