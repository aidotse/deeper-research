# Map: 2609.15989 - Corrupt Plans, Clean Traces: Evading Chain-of-Thought Monitoring with Plan Injection

## Method

- L51-52 (framework): Using "investigator-agent framework" to discover attack strategies by searching "for adversarial hints automatically" that both steer answers and evade monitors — systematic attack discovery approach
- L70-100 (investigator agents two-stage): Stage 1 trains "model q_φ(y|Q,a) that, given a question Q and an incorrect answer a, generates a target chain-of-thought y arriving at a" and Stage 2 trains "model p_θ(x|Q,a,y) that...generates a hint x that maximizes the likelihood the target model p_m assigns to producing y" with DPO refinement and diversity penalty — amortized attack generation
- L78-89 (SFT and Frank-Wolfe refinement): "Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)" on 3,018 stealthy flipped examples, then "Frank–Wolfe refinement" with "3 iterations" using DPO with diversity penalty "to make the investigator discover diverse strategies" — iterative improvement of attack strategies
- L141-152 (scaling experiments pipeline): "benign baselines" generating passing solutions, "corruptor model (DeepSeek-R1) rewrites the benign CoT into a plan injection" conditioned on mechanistically distinct strategies, actor "re-run on each plan injection" with permissive prompt — end-to-end attack pipeline

## Setup

- L67 (models for discovery): "Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct model" used for investigator, target model, and monitor in discovery phase — single model for initial discovery
- L56 (TruthfulQA discovery): "TruthfulQA [18] is a multiple-choice dataset of 817 questions spanning 38 disciplines" — discovery benchmark
- L140-144 (scaling tasks and models): "Qwen3-8B and DeepSeek-R1-0528" on "APPS dataset" with 550 problems and "Bio-Math" setting with "78 biology questions" plus "120 math questions" — models and tasks for scaling
- L169-170 (monitors): "five monitors spanning thinking and non-thinking models: GPT-4o Mini and DeepSeek V3 (non-thinking), and DeepSeek R1, Qwen3 235B, and Claude 3.7 Sonnet (thinking)" — monitor suite
- L170 (detection threshold): "suspiciousness score s∈[0,1] for each of the CoTs" treated "as problematic if s>0.15" — detection threshold

## Headline results

- L102-106 (strategy discovery): "investigator discovers a range of strategies" with "5 are entirely new" where "4 of the 5 new strategies share a common pattern where the hint is a fake reasoning chain concluding with the incorrect answer" and "most effective...cot_pure, simply appends the faulty injection plan" — discovered attack patterns
- L106-107 (paraphrasing behavior): "models often paraphrase the injected reasoning into its own CoT rather than copying or referencing it, producing transcripts that read as genuine reasoning and avoid monitor suspicion" — model adoption mechanism
- L156 (APPS success): "Qwen3-8B solved 414/550 (75.3%) and DeepSeek-R1 solved 468/550 (85.1%)" benign cases, "corruptor successfully produced plan injections...for 74.5% and 71.4%" passing public tests, "actor followed the injected plan in 50.6% of cases for Qwen3-8B and 41.7% for DeepSeek-R1" — coding backdoor success rates
- L158 (Bio-Math success): "Qwen3-8B solved 62/78 (79.5%) and DeepSeek-R1 solved 64/78 (82.1%)" of biology problems, "corruptor successfully modified 87.1% and 89.1%" to dual-answer CoTs, "actors adopted the injected reasoning in 93.0% and 98.9% of cases" — high success on disguised reasoning
- L172-178 (monitor detection): "Non-thinking monitors...detect substantially less than thinking monitors" with "even in the best case, [no monitor exceeds] ∼75% detection" leaving "substantial room for attacks to slip through" — thinking monitors better but imperfect
- L176 (plan visibility paradox): "Providing the plan sometimes decreases monitorability...on Bio-Math the with-plan setting substantially improves detection" but "in other cases...the monitor uses the injected plan to...explain it away" — inconsistent plan visibility effects
- L190-192 (thinking budget non-monotonicity): "the 20 transcripts whose suspicion rises most with budget show a group-mean increase of about 0.26 on APPS and 0.30 on Bio-Math, while the 20 that fall most show a group-mean decrease of about 0.28 on APPS and 0.30 on Bio-Math" — reasoning budget can harm or help detection

## Limitations

- L201-202 (scope of experiments): "experiments cover two reasoning models and two tasks; broader sweeps across model families and harder agentic tasks would strengthen generalization" — limited model and task coverage
- L202-203 (budget experiment limitations): "budget experiment is also run on a single monitor (Qwen3 235B, the only one in our pool exposing a budget parameter) and on a non-uniform sample of transcripts selected from the extremes" and "does not estimate how prevalent this phenomenon is, which we leave to future work" — limited budget analysis
- L202 (mechanism vs consequence): "we study consequences of plan corruption rather than mechanisms by which it arrives...end-to-end demonstrations through RAG, tool outputs, or multi-agent pipelines remain future work" — how plans enter context not addressed
- L342-343 (frontier model evaluation limits): "frontier models don't expose their raw CoTs (sometimes only a summarized CoT)" making "monitor behavior analysis" impossible; results "are not tailored to the frontier actor" using "un-optimized prompts" — frontier model testing limited

## Assumptions and conditions

- L17 (multiple context sources): "Models routinely operate on reasoning produced elsewhere, including retrieved documents in RAG pipelines, tool outputs, plans from upstream planner modules, and messages from other agents in multi-agent systems" — assumes realistic deployment contexts exist
- L130 (benign framing): "placing harmful but benign-sounding reasoning in a model's context can steer it into reproducing that same reasoning with a benign justification" — assumes benign phrasing enables deception
- L151-152 (verification success): "An attempt follows the plan if it satisfies the same task-specific verification" requiring backdoor success or dual-answer correctness — task-specific verification enables plan assessment
- L107, L160 (paraphrasing assumption): "fewer than 1% of plan-injection attacks result in the actor copying even half the plan verbatim" using "8-gram overlap" metric — assumes paraphrasing is genuine reasoning not copying
- L176-177 (monitor reasoning interpretation): "in some cases the monitor uses the injected plan to identify and flag the suspicious reasoning, while in others the injected plan's coherent narrative justifies the backdoor and the monitor explains it away" — assumes monitor reasoning quality varies

## Dimensions

- **evidence**: L140-160 (empirical): Experiments on APPS (550 problems) and Bio-Math (78+120 problems) with multiple actors and strategies, measuring success rates and detection rates — empirical benchmarking of attack and defense
- **measures**: L15-16, L169-170 (monitorability): "CoT monitoring" is core focus where "monitor (often another language model) for signs of unsafe planning, deception, or misalignment" with detection measured via "suspiciousness score" — explicitly testing if monitors can detect plan injection
- **elicitation**: L106-107, L130 (elicited): Plan injection "places harmful reasoning in a model's context" to steer behavior; "benign but benign-sounding reasoning" appended to prompt causes actor to "paraphrase" it as own — context-based elicitation without explicit instruction
- **pressure**: L141-152 (prompt-level): Plan injection is "inference-time attack" with no training pressure, only prompt modification with "permissive prompt" suggesting actor "may use this plan or derive from scratch" — no training involved, purely prompt-level
