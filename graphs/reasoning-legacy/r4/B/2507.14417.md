## Limitations and conditions

- "The majority of our tasks are synthetically generated to isolate specific flawed behaviors, which are useful for our
  analysis but may underestimate how these behaviors may manifest in real-world setups" (line 184) — author-stated
  limitation on naturalness.
- "We find that models maintain high accuracy with extended reasoning on standard arithmetic benchmarks" (line 76) —
  scope: inverse scaling was not found on MultiArith, ASDiv, GSM8K, GSM-IC or Inverse Scaling Prize tasks; the tasks
  were built to trigger it.
- "all three models—Claude Sonnet 4, o4-mini, and o3—do not exhibit inverse scaling across all four arithmetic tasks"
  (line 592) — same scope limit, with the note that those traces were under 1,000 reasoning tokens.
- "we observe no significant inverse scaling pattern across all three out-of-domain distractor types" (line 508) — the
  distraction effect needs in-domain distractors.
- "though providing few-shot examples largely corrects this behavior" (line 24) — condition on the spurious-feature
  result: it is a zero-shot effect.
- "In the zero-shot setting, models exhibit mixed scaling behaviors rather than uniform inverse scaling." (line 120) —
  the regression result is model-dependent; natural overthinking is mostly noisy or flat.
- "While trends across tasks are mostly weak and/or noisy, we focus our analysis on the Survival Instinct task, which we
  selected based on its clear scaling patterns" (line 150) — the self-preservation headline is one task out of 15,
  chosen for its clear pattern.
- "framed as a desire to assist users rather than self-preservation for its own sake" (line 180) — qualifies what the
  self-preservation result shows.
- "models interpret these controlled overthinking prompts with varying degrees of compliance" (line 324) — the reasoning
  length manipulation is imperfect; the authors "do not extensively tune the prompt".
- "though this may be partially explained by its tendency to generate shorter reasoning traces" (line 98) — o3's
  apparent stability may reflect shorter traces, not robustness.
- "a pattern not observed in the controlled overthinking setup" (line 98) — R1's 70%-to-30% drop depends on the natural
  setup.
- "OpenAI o-series models do not provide an option to disable thinking" (line 40) — o-series scaling is measured only
  across low, medium and high budgets.

## Other findings

- In Table 1, Zebra Puzzles under controlled overthinking are positive for the three Claude models and o3-mini, and noisy
  for the rest (line 62). Inverse scaling on deduction is universal only in natural overthinking (line 74). The draft's
  "all models degrade on complex deduction" needs that condition.
- Under controlled overthinking, Misleading Math shows positive scaling for o4-mini, Qwen3-32B and QwQ 32B (line 54). So
  the counting-task inverse scaling is mainly a Claude effect there.
