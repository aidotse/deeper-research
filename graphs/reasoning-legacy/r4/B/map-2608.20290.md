# Map: 2608.20290 — Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null

## Method

- L4-5 (title and framing): "Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null" — paper's central claim and method
- L19-22 (transition-level analysis): "tracking which enter and leave a model's reachable set and counting those destroyed under self-training... A transition is a difference between two estimates, each carrying sampling error, and the events of interest sit exactly where that error bites hardest" — problem being addressed
- L51-57 (ledger approach): "Fix an evaluation set P and checkpoints t=0,…,T, with t=0 the untrained base model... the corruption-to-learning ratio, CLR = |{p: s₀(p)=solved, sₜ(p)=unsolved}| / |{p: s₀(p)=unsolved, sₜ(p)=solved}|, problems destroyed per problem gained" — core measurement framework
- L61-65 (hysteresis discretization): "We instead treat π̂ₜ(p) as an estimator of the model's pass@1 probability and discretize with hysteresis" — how solve rate estimates are converted to binary states
- L72-75 (expansion rate definition): "Following Mayilvahanan et al. (2026), a problem that becomes pass@1-correct while the base model already solved it at least once in k samples has been sharpened; one the base model never reached has been expanded... ERₘ = |{p: π̂₀(p)=0 ∧ k π̂ₜ(p)≥m}| / |{p: π̂₀(p)=0}|" — expansion statistic and thresholding
- L79-81 (frozen control approach): "we re-evaluate a frozen model at every checkpoint and compute every statistic exactly as for a trained one; each transition it reports is an artifact by construction" — measurement control methodology
- L87 (training configurations): "All arms share the backbone (Qwen3-8B), rank-32 LoRA, three rounds of 256 problems from one stream, and evaluation at k=128" — standardized experimental setup

## Setup

- L87 (backbone and methods): "We audit STaR (Zelikman et al., 2022) (filter by ground-truth answer), majority-vote self-training in the manner of TTRL (Zuo et al., 2025)... and distillation from gpt-oss-120b (OpenAI et al., 2025) as a positive control" — methods evaluated
- L85 (benchmark suites): "MATH-500 (Lightman et al., 2024), subsampled to 200 problems... AIME 2025 and 2026 (60 problems)... A difficulty band of 1,163 problems, profiled from 10,999 held-out MATH problems" — three evaluation sets with different properties
- L85 (benchmark roles): "Corruption is observable only on problems the model already solves, expansion only on problems it does not reach; the two populations are disjoint" — why multiple benchmarks needed
- L87-88 (evaluation protocol): "All arms share... evaluation at k=128, T=0.8, top-p 0.95 plus one greedy sample per problem per checkpoint" — standard sampling configuration
- L148-151 (matched ladder): "one shared stream at matched volume, three seeds, so that only the correctness filter varies: the model's own majority vote, the ground-truth answer, an external teacher" — controlled comparison methodology

## Headline results

- L12 (auditing success): "seven measurement failures, each of which inverts a reported finding when its control is absent" — main finding about measurement artifacts
- L12 (single-decode problem): "a ledger built on a single greedy decode manufactures capability changes on an untrained model, largely an artifact of inference batching" — F1 finding
- L12 (expansion null): "The expansion statistic separating acquisition from sharpening assigns that same model a rate of 0.280" — F2 finding on null estimation
- L25-26 (ER null across replicates): "It returns 0.058 [0.038,0.078] over the 110 our experiments contain the null is... larger than the value it was supposed to certify" — expansion rate's actual null vs intended certification
- L26 (threshold-free test): "a per-problem exact test against a baseline pooled over every independent evaluation of the untrained model gives all arms a common denominator, returns no detection on any held-out replicate" — improved approach
- L27 (key dissociation): "Distillation improves 8–11 of the 22 problems the base model reaches at most five times in 1,408 draws, against 0–2 for three forms of self-training" — main capability finding
- L27 (statistical strength): "a logistic model rejects the reading that this merely follows from the teacher's larger overall gain (β=1.91, p<10⁻⁸)" — evidence against alternative explanations
- L27 (corruption observed): "both methods corrupt 88–106 of 1,163 band problems against a design-matched floor of 8" — corruption measurement
- L137-139 (seed instability): "Two further seeds on a matched 175-problem subsample invert the single-seed comparison... Majority-vote self-training is not: 0.55, 1.53, 1.33, with accuracy changes of +4.0, -2.8 and -1.0" — F5 finding on seed variability

## Limitations

- L200 (study scope): "Three rounds and roughly 270 optimizer steps of rank-32 LoRA, far short of the schedules we position against: what we establish is that this regime does not expand, not that self-training cannot" — limited training duration
- L200 (expansion scope): "Expansion rests on 22 low-base problems on one benchmark, and on the 10 the base model never reaches the comparison is not significant" — limited expansion population
- L200 (corruption context): "Corruption rests on a band drawn from MATH training problems almost certainly in the backbone's pretraining data and built to make corruption observable" — corruption measured on in-domain problems
- L200 (single backbone): "One backbone family carries every claim" — generalization limited to one model
- L295 (TTRL implementation): "Two deviations from TTRL as published. TTRL is reinforcement learning with a majority-vote reward, whereas the arm labeled majority-vote SFT below is cross-entropy fine-tuning on the rationales that agree with the vote" — methods may differ from original proposals
- L190-191 (bounded claims): "the refusal probe cannot resolve what it was built to measure: three replicates read -0.015, -0.115 and -0.005, the middle one significant and replicated by neither neighbor, so we report a bound" — safety measurement inconclusive

## Assumptions and conditions

- L78 (base-unreached definition): "π̂₀(p)=0 at k=128 does not establish π=0. We write 'base-unreached at k' rather than 'unsolvable' throughout" — key distinction about what measurements mean
- L78 (probability bounds): "no successes in 128 draws bounds the base pass@1 probability at q≤0.023 with 95% confidence, it does not establish q=0" — base rate estimation from zero successes
- L139-140 (seed pooling):  "A powered comparison at seed 0 finds no difference (p=0.406); pooling the two matched replicates for a Fisher test gives p=0.0076, and we do not report it, because pooling seeds as a fixed effect is what the same paragraph says three seeds cannot support" — seeds cannot be pooled as fixed effect
- L142-143 (null measurement): "every arm's checkpoint 0 is an independent evaluation of the untrained model... only at G=9 does it hold within ±0.02 of the value eleven give" — empirical null requires sufficient replicates
- L383 (regression to the mean): "selection uses an independent k=8 profile, the audit re-evaluates every problem from scratch at k=128, and that measurement is checkpoint 0... The noisy estimate used to choose never enters the ledger" — independence prevents bias from selection
- L127 (low-base stratum definition): "Restricted to the 22 problems whose pooled base rate is at most 5/1,408... Expansion is only interpretable in the two low-base columns" — claimed results specific to rare-success problems

## Dimensions

- **Evidence**: L21 (empirical measurement): "Auditing three rounds of rank-32 LoRA self-training on Qwen3-8B against a frozen control pushed through the identical pipeline, we identify seven measurement failures" — empirical audit of training runs with controlled comparisons

- **Measures**: L19 (problem-level metrics): "tracking which enter and leave a model's reachable set (Yuan et al., 2026) and counting those destroyed under self-training" — measures per-problem transitions in solved/unsolved states, not aggregate capability

- **Elicitation**: L87 (multiple training methods): "We audit STaR (filter by ground-truth answer), majority-vote self-training... and distillation from gpt-oss-120b (OpenAI et al., 2025) as a positive control" — multiple training procedures tested on same base model; positive control is external teacher

- **Pressure**: L81 (measurement control under identical conditions): "we measure how many by re-evaluating a frozen model at every checkpoint and computing every statistic exactly as for a trained one; each transition it reports is an artifact by construction" — tests measurement error under same pipeline; no adversarial pressure on methods but measurement infrastructure provides control
