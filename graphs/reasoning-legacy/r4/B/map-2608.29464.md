# Map for 2608.29464

## Method
- L4-5 (Title): "Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness of Reasoning Models Varies with Where and How Preference Cues Are Delivered" — the paper's main claim about CoT faithfulness variation
- L11 (FACE-Eval name): "We introduce FACE-Eval (Faithful Attribution of Cue Effects Evaluation), a 5,100-sample evaluation that varies cue delivery location (user message or tool return) and its explicitness (direct summary or raw artifact)" — the evaluation methodology
- L23-24 (Verbalized commitment rate): "We measure the verbalized commitment rate (VCR): the fraction of cue-following answers whose CoT explicitly verbalizes the decision to tailor the response to the preference." — the key metric
- L49-52 (Metric definition): "We define mathrm{Align}_mathrm{ans} when the answer follows the cued preference, mathrm{Commit}_mathrm{CoT} when the trace states an intent to tailor, and mathrm{cued} when a sample contains a cue." — formal metric definitions
- L35 (Dataset composition): "We introduce FACE-Eval, which contains 5,000 cued samples and 100 no-cue controls. The cued samples cross five preference axes, two predefined sides per axis, five sources, five cue-delivery conditions, and 20 scenarios per axis" — evaluation dataset structure

## Setup
- L35-44 (Dataset axes): "The five axes (and their sides) are political (liberal v.s. conservative), ethics (utilitarian v.s. deontological), egalitarianism (egalitarian v.s. elitist), epistemic posture (skeptical v.s. deferential), and domain expertise (expert v.s. novice)." — preference dimensions tested
- L44-45 (Sources for cues): "The five sources are the types of artifact that carry the cue: profile (a short profile summarizing the user's overall preference), email (recent email conversations), slack (recent Slack messages), notes (personal notes), and browser_history (recent browsing)." — data sources for preference cues
- L45 (Models evaluated): "Our analyses report 15 open-weight models from eight model families, three seeds each, under three system prompts... Qwen 3.5 dense... Gemma 4... OLMo 3 Think... GPT-OSS... DeepSeek V4... GLM 5.2... Kimi K2.6... and Inkling" — model specifications
- L45 (Model scale): "The set spans 4B to 1.60T total parameters, with five models at or above 284B." — parameter range
- L40-41 (Channel conditions): "The first axis, Channel, defines whether the cue is in the user message or a tool return. The second, explicitness, defines whether the cue states the preference directly or leaves it to be inferred from raw artifacts." — experimental conditions

## Headline results
- L27 (Main finding on explicitness): "All evaluated models verbalize commitment more often for explicit than implicit cues and for user-message than tool-return cues." — headline claim about VCR patterns
- L27-28 (Unverbalized adoption finding): "Unverbalized adoption is higher for tool-return cues on every model and for implicit cues in 28 of 30 model–channel comparisons." — finding about silent preference following
- L28 (System prompt testing): "The source-attribution prompt narrows the channel gap on seven models, though sometimes by increasing user-channel unverbalized adoption rather than reducing it on the tool channel." — system prompt effects
- L11 (Monitor correlation): "Across the 32 evaluated model–channel–explicitness cells, higher unverbalized adoption is associated with worse ability to detect verbalization for both monitors (correlations of -0.54 for GPT-5.6-Luna and -0.78 for GPT-4o-mini)." — monitor detection degradation

## Limitations
- L265-266 (Monitor evaluation scope): "Our analyses report 15 open-weight models from eight model families" — limited to open-weight models
- L268 (Preference axis coverage): "Although we make efforts to use preference axes that represent important considerations for AI oversight and are grounded in existing research, we are unable to test all possible axes or their weightings." — incomplete preference axis coverage
- L271-272 (Causality limitation): "While our planted-cue setup helps ground evaluation in realistic scenarios, a planted cue is not identical to a user communicating a preference through tool returns in deployment." — evaluation artificiality
- L272 (Generalization concern): "It remains unclear to what extent the patterns we observe generalize to other preference domains or model families not covered in our evaluation." — generalization uncertainty

## Assumptions and conditions
- L17 (Core CoT monitoring assumption): "Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring rests on the assumption that reasoning traces faithfully record relevant information shaping a model's answer, including decisions to act on a preference" — central assumption being tested
- L39-40 (Matched cue condition): "For each scenario and preferred side, the explicit and implicit cues express the same underlying preference. At a fixed explicitness level, the user-message and tool-return conditions use identical cue text." — controlled comparison condition
- L50-51 (Eligibility filtering): "A transcript is eligible if its answer is judged to take a stance and its matched no-cue response for the same scenario, model, and seed is uncommitted" — filtering condition for analysis
- L41-42 (Tool format): "The five cue-delivery conditions vary channel role (i.e., user message v.s. tool return) and cue explicitness (prose summary v.s. raw artifact). For each scenario and preferred side, the explicit and implicit cues express the same underlying preference." — comparison design constraints

## Dimensions

### Evidence
- L11 (Result type): "We measure how often cue-following answers verbalize this decision, and how often all cued samples follow the preference without doing so. We evaluate 15 open-weight models from eight families" — empirical measurement across models

### Measures
- L23-24 (Measurement focus): "We measure the verbalized commitment rate (VCR): the fraction of cue-following answers whose CoT explicitly verbalizes the decision to tailor the response to the preference." — measures monitorability (whether CoT records decision to follow cue)
- L49 (Distinguishing from other metrics): "Verbalized commitment is also distinct from causal dependence: it asks whether the trace reports a decision to use the cue, not whether intervening on the CoT changes the answer." — specific monitorability framing

### Elicitation
- L18-19 (Planted cue setup): "A common planted-cue setup inserts a biased hint into the user message and checks whether the model mentions it when its answer changes" — elicited through explicit planted preferences
- L19-20 (Tool channel elicitation): "Agents also retrieve user memories, emails, and environmental observations through tools. In agentic systems, preference cues may therefore reach the model through a tool return rather than the user message" — preference elicitation through tools
- L39-40 (Implicit vs explicit elicitation): "The two explicit conditions carry the same prose summary across channels; the two implicit conditions carry the same raw artifact." — manipulation of how preferences are presented

### Pressure
