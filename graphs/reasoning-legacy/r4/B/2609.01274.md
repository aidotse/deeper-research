## Cells

- `method name`: confirmed — Unified Decoding Framework (UDF, also "SearchLens"); Budgeted Operating-Point Transition Rule (BOPTR; the pass@k instance is BOPTR-P1, also "SearchPath") — "UDF (SearchLens), a unified budgeted operating space for external decoding and search policies, separated from evaluation metrics." (L32); "BOPTR-P1 (SearchPath), a low-dimensional pass@ $k$ rule" (L40)
- `method summary`: confirmed, with additions — UDF wraps a frozen base model in a five-component policy (local policy, controller, evaluator, transition, scheduler); each policy plus a budget is an operating point (π, n). Metrics are applied only after generation. Recovery asks whether the RL default-policy curve lies inside the Base+UDF envelope, then whether the near-matches form a low-complexity path. BOPTR-P1 predicts the Base budget as N̂ = round(α·b^β), with β tied to the benchmark regime (math 0.60, nonmath/OOD 1.00, floor 0.00), log α = μ_regime + δ_m (a model-specific scalar offset), and the policy chosen by a selector loss. The anchor cell is Qwen2.5-7B/Math500; budgets are {1,2,4,8,16}. — "BOPTR-P1, the pass@ $k$ instance, uses two structural assumptions: the budget exponent $\beta_{g(d)}$ is tied to the benchmark regime $g(d)$ , while model-specific variation is captured by a scalar offset $\delta_{m}$ ." (L202); "with budgets $1,2,4,8,16$" (L136)
- `datasets / models`: confirmed, with additions — Math500, AIME 2024/2025, GPQA-Diamond, IFEval; held-out AMC23, Minerva, OlympiadBench, TinyMMLU. Seven SimpleRL-Zoo Base/RL pairs (Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/7B/14B, Qwen2.5-Math-7B, Llama3.1-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.1), plus three non-SimpleRL-Zoo RL checkpoints added after fitting (Open-Reasoner-Zero-7B, OAT-7B, DeepSeek-Math-7B), for ten models across four families. — "We analyze paired Base/RL checkpoints from SimpleRL-Zoo, including Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/7B/14B, Qwen2.5-Math-7B, Llama3.1-8B, and Mistral-7B-v0.1" (L128); "Open-Reasoner-Zero-7B, OAT-7B, and DeepSeek-Math-7B give 4.87, 4.48, and 3.28 pp" (L333)
- `metric`: confirmed — transfer error mech_P = mean |P̂_RL − P_RL| in percentage points of pass@k over budgets 1-16 — "We measure rule quality by $\mathrm{mech}_{P}\equiv|\widehat{P}_{\mathrm{RL}}-P_{\mathrm{RL}}|\cdot 100$ in percentage points." (L222)
- `result`: corrected (conditions missing) — All numbers are confirmed in the body: 3.41 pp on Qwen2.5-7B (95% CI [2.32, 5.53]); 3.07 ± 0.39 pp over three seeds; 3.28-4.87 pp on the three added checkpoints; 5.03 vs 4.44 pp on the four unfitted benchmarks; 4.19 pp with a base-only offset; 5.08 pp with no RL information. The caveats are missing. "Lowest among non-oracle rules" is a point-estimate claim: base greedy (H1, mean 3.70 pp) is at statistical parity (−0.04 pp, CI [−2.69, +2.50]). Direct anchor-path transfer reaches 10.16 pp on Llama. Extending budgets to 64/256 raises error to 5.02 pp, with 12.50 pp on AIME. — "whereas H1 is at parity ( $-0.04$ pp, $[-2.69,+2.50]$ )" (L234); "raises the mean error to 5.02 pp, concentrated on AIME at 12.50 pp" (L234); "It degrades for the 1.5B downscale and for the cross-family Llama setting, where the error reaches 10.16 pp." (L323)

## Limitations and conditions

- "This is not a claim that the RL model literally executes any external decoding policy; we make no parameter-level mechanistic identification." (L393) — the "internalised search" reading is behavioural only.
- "Recoverability statements should be interpreted relative to the UDF policies actually evaluated in each cell" (L397) — some cells have only autoregressive policy pools; AIME has only 6 policies in its pool (L234).
- "Cross-benchmark numbers should be read with this regime structure in mind, not as a uniform metric." (L401) — IFEval and GPQA use different metric semantics.
- "The predictor still depends on a cohort of RL models rather than a single target, and its behavior beyond the tested cohort is not established." (L405) — the "no RL checkpoint for the target" result needs a cohort of RL models.
- "We do not claim our regime map ( $\beta_{\text{math}}{=}0.6$ , $\beta_{\text{nonmath}}{=}1.0$ , $\beta_{\text{floor}}{=}0$ ) transfers verbatim to other RL recipes" (L409) — the fitted exponents are recipe-specific.
- "Training-duration effects at the scale of prolonged RL training [Liu et al., 2025] remain untested; our claims are confined to the recipes and training durations evaluated." (L409) — the qualified internalised-search reading does not cover prolonged RL, which other work reports expands the boundary (L58).
- "The number of regimes (3) is likewise determined by the four available benchmarks and the minimal AIME sample size" (L409); "refitting AIME into the math regime ( $\beta=0.60$ ) changes the AIME cell error by $+1.67$ pp with a 95% CI of $[-5.00,+4.00]$ that contains zero" (L228) — the AIME floor regime is not distinguishable from the math regime.
- "Extending the budget grid to $b=64$ , and to $b=256$ on AIME, raises the mean error to 5.02 pp" (L234) — the headline fit holds over budgets 1-16 only.
- "all four benchmark means stay under the pre-registered 8 pp criterion, although five individual cells exceed it" (L333) — held-out transfer fails in some cells.
- "BOPTR is best used as a diagnostic of RL–Base relationships, not as a universal post-RL performance predictor." (L383)
- "This suggests that the shared RL-training distribution is important for direct $(\pi,N)$ transfer" (L323) — direct transfer is conditioned on the same Hard RL-training split.

## Other findings

- The comparison baseline matters for the 3.41 pp headline. Base greedy alone (H1) scores 3.70 pp mean and is statistically tied with BOPTR-P1 (L234, Table 2B at L249-257). BOPTR's clear wins are over same-policy, anchor-copy and strict-β rules. This weakens how much the specific power-law rule, rather than the base model's ordinary behaviour, explains the RL curve.
- For cluster C, the paper sides with "select / sampling efficiency" but only in qualified form. It says "much of the measured RL gain corresponds to a change in sampling efficiency" under the recipe tested (L14). It also notes explicitly that prolonged RL may expand the reasoning boundary, outside its scope (L58, L409).
- The anchor cell alone required about 461 H100 GPU-hours (L136); the full landscape with expanded controllers was built only for the anchor.
