## Limitations and conditions

- "our main checkpoint is trained for only 47000 steps on a broadly untested mixture, and the learning rate is never cooled down from its peak" (L260) — all benchmark results come from one under-trained, un-cooled checkpoint.
- "the 800B token count, while large in comparison to older fully open-source models such as the Pythia series, is small in comparison to modern open-source efforts such as OLMo" (L260) — limits the comparison to other models.
- "due to compute and time constraints, we were not able to ablate this mixture" (L170) — the data mixture, skewed to code and math, is untested; a single large run.
- "but lags behind the later OLMo models trained larger, more carefully curated datasets" (L320) — the headline is not state of the art at its size class.
- "although it does not outperform dedicated code models, such as StarCoder2" (L338) — scope of the coding claim.
- "Intuitively this makes sense, as the recurrent models has less capacity to memorize facts but more capacity to reason about its context." (L378) — closed-book knowledge tasks are a weakness; OBQA improves only when a relevant fact is given.
- "At small scales all normalization strategies worked, and we observed only tiny differences between initializations. The same was not true at scale." (L244) — two of three large runs failed (representation collapse, or learning to ignore the recurrent state); the result depends on a specific sandwich-norm, initialization and learning-rate setup.
- "validation perplexity is the same whether 1 or 32 recurrences are used" (L248) — the failed second run shows recurrence is not automatically used; test-time scaling is conditional on the training configuration.
- "We verify at small scale that this modification improves compute utilization without impacting convergence speed" (L186) — locked-step depth sampling was only checked at small scale.
- "As a preliminary demonstration, we verify on MTBench that this adaptivity does not significantly impact performance" (L394) — the adaptive-exit claim is preliminary.
- "we note that there is concern that this comes with costs in model oversight in comparison to verbalized chains of thought, that are currently still human-readable" (L494) — the authors flag the monitorability cost of latent reasoning.
- "there are many questions that remain unanswered" (L450) — post-training schemes (RL, compressing the recurrence, internalising CoT) are untested.

## Other findings

- The "50B parameters" figure is a compute (FLOP) equivalence for test-time scaling, not a claim of matching a 50B model's accuracy; the draft's `metric` "accuracy vs equivalent parameter count" misreads it.
- The only controlled comparison is the 180B-token fixed-depth baseline; comparisons to Pythia/OLMo confound architecture with data and token count.
- No comparison against explicit chain-of-thought at matched compute exists in the mapped text; the GSM8K CoT setting has the model itself emitting CoT tokens, so latent recurrence and verbalised CoT are combined there, not contrasted.
- Results gains are task-dependent: easier tasks (HellaSwag, OBQA) saturate around 8 recurrences, while GSM8K keeps improving (L340, L34).
