# Location map: 2609.01274
## From Base Rollouts to RL Reasoning: A Budgeted Search Perspective

## Method

- L7-19 (Abstract): "We study this behaviorally with a Unified Decoding Framework (UDF), which expresses token-level sampling, beam-like search, tree search, and sequence-level resampling as executable policies over a shared budgeted operating space" — core methodological framework
- L100-130 (Section 3, SearchLens UDF): UDF represents each method as operating point u=(π,n) where π∈Π_UDF is policy configuration and n∈B is inference budget; policies consist of Local policy (proposal, gating, scoring), Controller (autoregressive, beam, branching, tree, resampling), Evaluator (likelihood, rollout utility, verifier), Transition (appending, expanding, branching), and Scheduler (budget allocation)
- L138-150 (Policy Geometry): Associates each operating point with feature vector z(u)=[P(u),Q(u),T(u),D(u),C(u)] measuring pass@k, SC, first-finish/validity, diversity, and cost; defines distance d(u_i,u_j) combining component, behavioral, and budget distances
- L186-230 (Section 5 Analysis): Same-policy comparison defines ΔSame^c(b)=A_RL^c(π₀,b)-A_Base^c(π₀,b) testing whether RL changes behavior under fixed policy and budget; Base+UDF recovery tests whether RL default-policy curve lies inside base model's decoding/search landscape
- L260-330 (Section 6 SearchPath/BOPTR): "Budgeted Operating-Point Transition Rule" formally predicts RL pass rate through Base operating point using N̂(b)=round_B(α_{m,d}^ch × b^{β_{g(d)}^ch}); selects policy π̂=argmin_π L where L is selector loss

## Setup

- L156-175 (Section 4 Experimental Setup): Anchor cell is Qwen2.5-7B on Math500 for full landscape construction; paired Base/RL checkpoints from SimpleRL-Zoo including Qwen2.5 (0.5B/1.5B/7B/14B), Qwen2.5-Math-7B, Llama3.1-8B, Mistral-7B-v0.1
- L175-185 (Benchmarks and Policies): Evaluates Math500, AIME 2024/2025, GPQA-Diamond, and IFEval; token-level autoregressive policies and expanded search policies (MCMC-like resampling, entropy branching, tree controllers) with budgets {1,2,4,8,16}
- L183-188: "Anchor cell requires approximately 461 H100 GPU-hours, dominated by eight expanded-controller runs. Generations are deduplicated at b=16 and reused across smaller budgets and all four metrics"
- L185: "Our main analyses use same-policy curves, Base+UDF envelopes, near-match policies, and low-complexity rule families; tables report MAE in percentage points relative to the RL default-policy target"

## Headline results

- L230-236 (Same-Policy Gains, Figure 3): "Base catches up only at larger budgets: the number of Base-shared policies with Base ≥ RL is 0/24, 0/24, 0/24, 2/24, and 13/24 across increasing budgets" — RL dominates at low budgets, Base recovers at high budgets
- L265-293 (BOPTR Regime Discovery, Table 3B): "BOPTR-P1 with regime-specific β is the strongest non-oracle rule, reducing the mean error from 6.01 pp for anchor per-budget copy to 3.41 pp" with 95% CI [2.32, 5.53]; three-seed replication gives 3.07±0.39 pp
- L295-312 (Regime Map): "Math500 is sublinear [β=0.60], AIME fits β=0 [floor regime], GPQA and IFEval are approximately linear [β=1.00, nonmath/OOD regime]" — benchmark-conditioned exponents
- L335-345 (Vertical Transfer): Within same Hard RL-training split: 7B/14B/Math-7B obtain errors of 1.40/2.55/3.73 pp; degrades for 1.5B and cross-family Llama where error reaches 10.16 pp
- L350-360 (V3 Offset Baseline): "one-dimensional offset reduces the overall error from 5.36 to 4.44 pp" and "improvement is especially pronounced in the lower cohort, where the mean error drops from 6.30 to 4.21 pp"
- L380-410 (Coverage): Frozen rule on three RL checkpoints outside SimpleRL-Zoo gives 4.87/4.48/3.28 pp; on four held-out benchmarks (AMC23, Minerva, OlympiadBench, TinyMMLU) gives 5.03 pp against 4.44 pp in fit

## Limitations

- L463-469 (Behavioral, not parameter-level equivalence): "This is not a claim that the RL model literally executes any external decoding policy; we make no parameter-level mechanistic identification"
- L471-474 (Policy-pool comparability): "Some settings expose AR-only pools while others include expanded controllers"; "Recoverability statements should be interpreted relative to the UDF policies actually evaluated in each cell"
- L476-489 (Metric heterogeneity): "IFEval is treated as an instruction-following and termination stress test"; different behavioral channels (pass@k, SC, best-of-N, FFS) have different semantics across benchmarks
- L485-489: "Cross-family degradation delimits this interpretation, and predicting δ_m still relies on a cohort of RL models rather than on the target model alone: BOPTR is best used as a diagnostic of RL–Base relationships, not as a universal post-RL performance predictor"

## Assumptions and conditions

- L19-33 (Core behavioral hypothesis): "Does RL produce reasoning behavior the base model does not have, or does it shift the rollout distribution toward trajectories the base model can already reach but rarely samples?" — assumes RL primarily reallocates existing capabilities
- L42-60 (Search budget assumption): "A decoding or search method is an inference-time policy that maps a prompt to candidate responses"; implicitly assumes finite rollout budget and fixed policy structure
- L100-138 (UDF separation): Evaluation metrics "are applied only after generation" and "the generation policy stays separate from the evaluation metric"; this separation enables asking whether RL curves can be recovered without assuming same-budget comparison
- L260-330 (BOPTR structural form): Assumes β_g(d) is tied to benchmark regime g(d) while model-specific variation captured by scalar offset δ_m; assumes log α_{m,d}=μ_{g(d)}+δ_m decomposition
- L380-410 (Transfer assumptions): Assumes "regime exponents and model offsets should be re-estimated rather than assumed to transfer unchanged" for other post-training recipes

## Dimensions

- L7-19 (evidence): `empirical` — paper measures observable behavior of base/RL pairs: "pass@ k , self-consistency, best-of- N , and first-finish success"; results are benchmark measurements on specific model checkpoints, not theoretical proofs
- L19-33, L186-236 (measures): `capability` — measures reasoning task performance (pass rates); also measures `monitorability` in sense that structured search paths track what RL achieves, making internal allocation of attention monitorable
- L42-60, L156-175 (elicitation): Both `elicited` (RL models trained with RLVR on verifiable rewards) and `observational` (analyzing existing Base/RL pairs without constructing new training); Base model behavior is `spontaneous` (emerges from pretraining)
- L186-330 (pressure): `prompt-level` — external search policies applied at inference time without training; some methods like MCMC-resampling and tree search exert exploration pressure; contrasts with training-time RL pressure in the RL model itself
