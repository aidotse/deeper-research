- `method name`: confirmed — FACE-Eval (Faithful Attribution of Cue Effects Evaluation) — "We introduce FACE-Eval (Faithful Attribution of Cue Effects Evaluation), a 5,100-sample evaluation" (L11)
- `method summary`: corrected — Cue-injection faithfulness evaluation. A preference cue is planted either in the user message or in a prefilled single-call tool return (identical cue text within an explicitness level), and stated either explicitly (prose summary) or implicitly (raw artifact). The primary metric, VCR, is the fraction of cue-following answers whose CoT records a decision to tailor the answer to the preference; this is not whether the CoT mentions the cue, and not causal dependence. Unverbalised adoption (UAR) is the fraction of all eligible cued samples whose answer follows the cue while the trace records no such decision; eligibility requires the matched no-cue control to be uncommitted. The draft's gloss "the cue shifted the answer without being mentioned" is wrong on both counts. Two system-prompt variants (source-attribution directive, monitor-awareness disclosure) are tested. Separately, a transcript monitor scores tailoring on the largest model of each family. — "We instead ask whether the CoT states an intent to tailor the response toward the preference." (L49); "it asks whether the trace reports a decision to use the cue, not whether intervening on the CoT changes the answer" (L23); "Because the model does not select the prefilled tool call, this setting evaluates continuation after a tool result rather than tool-selection policy." (L41)
- `datasets / models`: confirmed, with additions — FACE-Eval: 5,000 cued samples plus 100 no-cue controls (5 axes x 2 sides x 5 sources x 5 conditions x 20 scenarios per axis); scenario prose generated by Claude Opus 4.6 and hand-reviewed. 15 open-weight models from eight families (Qwen 3.5, Gemma 4, OLMo 3 Think, GPT-OSS, DeepSeek V4, GLM 5.2, Kimi K2.6, Inkling), 4B to 1.60T, three seeds, three system prompts. Monitors GPT-5.6-Luna and GPT-4o-mini, run only on the largest model of each family (8 models, 27B-1.60T); detection labels from Claude Haiku 4.5. — "Our analyses report 15 open-weight models from eight model families, three seeds each, under three system prompts" (L45); "We evaluate the monitor on the largest model of each of the eight families, all under the default system prompt." ## Limitations and conditions

- "These results are descriptive measurements in the single-call, prefilled-tool setting tested here." (L155) — all results are scoped to one response after a prefilled tool call.
- "Our experiments show where verbalized commitment changes, but they do not identify why." (L159) — no mechanism for the channel or explicitness gaps.
- "The lower $\mathit{VCR}$ for tool-return cues could reflect training priors, different ways of reporting user- and tool-provided information, or judge sensitivity to channel-specific phrasing." (L161) — the channel gap may partly be a judge artefact.
- "The explicit and implicit cues also differ in format, directness, and inference burden." (L161) — the explicitness comparison is not a clean manipulation.
- "Tailoring the answer may not necessarily require multi-step reasoning, especially the explicit samples, so models may use the trace as an optional report rather than as a necessary workspace." (L163) — results may not transfer to tasks needing serial reasoning; "Our results may therefore differ for tasks where applying the cue requires substantial serial reasoning." (L163)
- "In the tool-channel conditions, each model continues after a prefilled tool call and return; it does not choose the call itself." (L163) — no tool-selection policy is tested.
- "$\mathit{VCR}$ depends on an automated judge. The second judge reproduces the main trends, but absolute values and some model rankings differ" (L165) — magnitudes are judge-dependent; "We therefore emphasize the signs of within-model comparisons rather than differences in magnitude between families." (L165)
- "Both judges apply the same rubric and see the same condition metadata, so their agreement does not rule out shared, channel-specific rubric error" (L165) — judges see condition metadata.
- "Longer traces may also give the judge more opportunities to find commitment language" (L165) — confound for the reasoning-effort results.
- "These estimates are not causal effects of cue form. The evaluated models rate their own cues, so measurement error weakens the matching." (L471) — the clarity-matched explicitness gap rests on self-ratings.
- "Unlike the primary rates, the monitor evaluation uses all transcripts before the eval-awareness filter" (L107) — monitor and primary rates use different transcript sets.
- "Each model fit uses only four conditions and is descriptive." (L117) — per-model correlations are on four points.
- "Each channel–explicitness condition reuses the same no-cue controls, so the four AUROC estimates are correlated." (L111)
- "Several intervals lie near zero, so the exact count is sensitive to bootstrap resampling." (L129) — the monitor-awareness model counts are fragile.
- "A narrowed gap is therefore not, by itself, a monitorability gain." (L127) — condition on reading the directive result.

## Other findings

- The monitor scores whether the assistant tailored its answer, so it detects preference adoption. The abstract's phrase "ability to detect verbalization" (L11) does not match the body's definition (L109). A row should describe the monitor as detecting adoption.
- Absolute gaps in unverbalised adoption are small. Under the default prompt the mean explicit-to-implicit increase is 0.048 (user) and 0.053 (tool) (L131), and the monitor AUROC differences across conditions span only 0.71-0.73 for Luna (L115). The VCR gaps are much larger (up to 0.77, L27).
- With the single disclosure tested, the authors "do not find the erosion that Korbak et al. (2025) warn about" (L129). This is a negative result on monitor-awareness that the draft's phrasing ("does not reliably close the gap") only partly conveys.
- On GPT-OSS, higher reasoning effort raises VCR in every condition without closing either gap (L29); the draft omits the effort analysis.
