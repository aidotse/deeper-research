## Limitations and conditions

- "When applying TTS , researchers typically choose a specific “what to scale” based on an empirical hypothesis, aiming to achieve performance gains." (L47) — the taxonomy's first axis organises methods by hypotheses that are empirical, not established.
- "The effectiveness of parallel scaling depends on both coverage—the likelihood of generating at least one correct response—and aggregation quality" (L55) — the condition under which parallel scaling helps.
- "Empirically, this relationship is often log-linear with respect to compute (Brown et al., 2024)." (L55) — the scaling-law framing is an empirical regularity cited from others, not derived.
- "suggesting that purely sequential approaches, while effective, may be only one part of a broader solution." (L63) — scope limit on sequential scaling.
- "internal scaling lacks clear intermediate checkpoints, posing challenges for debugging and efficiency management." (L122) — stated limitation of internal scaling.
- "Are there fundamental constraints on how much test-time scaling can improve reasoning performance?" (L142) — the authors flag that the mathematical properties and limits of TTS are open.
- "Re-evaluating Reward Modeling: whether PRMs actually improve multi-step inference?" (L138) — whether a core technique works is posed as open.
- "As empirical observations on certain property models (xAI, 2025) show blindly scaling over test-time may lead to over-thinking." (L150) — more test-time compute is not always beneficial.
- "This concept highlights how excessively long reasoning chains may not yield proportionate gains in solution quality" (L557) — efficiency caveat on longer reasoning.
- "Balancing Cost and Accuracy: Unlike general NLP tasks, specialized domains often require strict computational efficiency and reliability;" (L166) — limits on transfer to specialised domains.
- "We plan to continuously update our taxonomy to reflect ongoing progress" (L39) — the taxonomy is a living, versioned artefact (this text is v3), so its categories may change.
