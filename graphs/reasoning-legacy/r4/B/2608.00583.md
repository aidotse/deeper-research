## Limitations and conditions

- "The live-agent study bounds the spontaneous threat on 27 tasks from one task family and one benchmark" (line 157) —
  scope of the live-agent result.
- "The low-scoring residual rests on counts of 7, 7 and 2, too few to fix as a rate" (line 157) — the spontaneous
  blindspot cannot be quantified.
- "so the calibrated threshold lands on a tie and the realized false-positive rate is 8 to 26%, not 5%" (line 157) —
  condition on the live-agent "catches nearly all" result.
- "for claude-opus three hacks sit exactly on the threshold, so its 0 of 38 depends on flagging scores that equal the
  95th percentile" (line 157) — the claude-opus zero-miss count is fragile.
- "the AUC is 0.92 to 0.98 and 5 of 38, 13 of 68 and 2 of 21 hacks evade" (line 157) — against a different negative
  class (non-hollow solves under the hack instruction), the live-agent result weakens.
- "The live run does not apply the rewrite to live hacks" (line 157) — the collapse is shown only on the frozen pool.
- "We study a single benchmark and one monitor-rubric family with LLM-judge monitors" (line 157) — generalisation.
- "The white-box probe runs on an open-weight surrogate, since the proprietary monitors expose no activations" (line 157)
  — the probe result is not on the monitors evaluated.
- "We do not test an adaptive attacker that re-optimizes against a specific defense" (line 157) — the defence results
  (e.g. 48% with a reference solution, 20% for the primed monitor) are not stress-tested adaptively.
- "monitor endpoints drift over time, so we report the collapse, which is large and one-sided, and treat fine-grained
  point deltas as approximate" (line 157) — precision of reported numbers.
- "Throughout, “reasoning” means the agent’s emitted reasoning trace, the channel a black-box monitor reads, which can
  differ from the model’s hidden extended thinking" (line 75) — scope: emitted narration, not hidden thinking.
- "Cost caps the paid runs, so the attack is scored on a fixed random draw of 209 of these hacks" (line 71) — sample
  sizes per table.

## Other findings

- The pooled attack failed the authors' own pre-registered success threshold (30-point M0 drop). The paper's point is
  that this pooled view is the misleading one, but a reader of the row should know it.
- Trace-only defences (skeptic, consistency, oracle-localized, intent-blind monitors) all stay at the floor; an
  obfuscation-aware primed monitor recovers 20%; a reference solution recovers 48% (lines 101-107). The draft mentions
  none of this.
- The paper frames the fragility as "latent": unaided live agents' hacks are mostly action-visible and caught. The
  collapse needs the rewrite (line 135).
