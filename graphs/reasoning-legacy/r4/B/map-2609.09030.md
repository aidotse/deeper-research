## Method
- L57-61: "Given a query $Q$, a reasoning model with parameters $\theta$ generates a sequence of intermediate reasoning tokens $C_{1:K}=(C_{1},\dots,C_{K})$ followed by an answer sequence $A_{1:T}=(A_{1},\dots,A_{T})$. The corresponding autoregressive distribution over reasoning traces is given by $p_{\theta}(C_{1:K}\mid Q)=\prod_{k=1}^{K}p_{\theta}(C_{k}\mid Q,C_{1:k-1})$" — defines model generating reasoning traces and answer sequences.
- L63-65: "For a prefix $C_{1:k}$, the prefix-conditioned predictive distribution over discrete answer labels is $p_{k}(a)=p_{\theta}(Y=a\mid Q,C_{1:k})$, which marginalizes over all possible future reasoning continuations" — estimates predictive distribution over answers conditioned on reasoning prefix.
- L69-71: "For a question $q$ and realized reasoning trace $c_{1:K}$, we define the answer-distribution trajectory as $\mathcal{T}(q,c)=\left(p_{0},p_{1},\ldots,p_{K}\right)$, where $p_{k}$ denotes the prefix-conditioned predictive distribution associated with the reasoning prefix $c_{1:k}$" — formalizes answer-distribution trajectories as sequences of distributions.
- L113-134: "We measure exploration by effective support, revision by dominance switches and recurrence, motion by total-variation path length and directness, and commitment by when a hypothesis becomes stable" — introduces four dimensions for trajectory-level metrics.

## Setup
- L189: "We evaluate sixteen models across four datasets (GSM8K, ARC, SVAMP, and MATH) spanning base, instruction-tuned, and RL-trained regimes" — evaluates diverse set of models on four reasoning benchmarks.
- L293-323: "Sixteen open-weight LLMs corresponding to different training regimes: Gemma-2 (base and IT), LLaMA-3.2 and 3.1 (base and IT), Qwen-2.5 (base and IT), Qwen-2.5-Math-1.5B (SFT), DeepSeek-Chat-7B (SFT), DeepSeek-R1-distilled-7B (RL), Yi-1.5-34B (base)" — list of sixteen models with different training approaches.
- L327-347: "For each question $Q=q$, we sample $M$ independent reasoning trajectories from the model under a fixed stochastic decoding configuration (temperature, nucleus sampling, maximum generation length), with temperature $T=0.7$, nucleus sampling with $p=0.9$, and maximum generation length of 600 tokens" — decoding configuration for trajectory sampling.
- L357-369: "For each fixed prefix $(q,c_{1:k})$, we draw $N$ independent stochastic continuations from the model. The samples define the empirical prefix-conditioned answer distribution $\widehat{p}_{k}(a)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{1}\!\left\{Y^{(i)}=a\right\}$. We use $N=16$ independent continuations per prefix unless otherwise stated" — Monte Carlo estimation of prefix-conditioned distributions with 16 continuations.

## Headline results
- L197-198: "Models with similar endpoint accuracy can nevertheless exhibit markedly different mixtures of success and failure mechanisms, as seen among neighboring rows in the accuracy-ordered panels" — similar accuracy can hide different reasoning dynamics.
- L209: "Even for traces with similar entropy profiles, trajectories can disagree about which answers are dominant, whether the gold answer is dominant, and which success or failure mechanism the trajectory instantiates" — entropy profiles don't capture full dynamics.
- L219-220: "Every reported variance component is nonzero, indicating heterogeneity in each metric. Notably, the within-condition component is the largest for every metric, which indicates that a model-task pair does not correspond to a single characteristic reasoning profile" — substantial within-model variation in reasoning dynamics.
- L237: "When the goal is efficiency, the shortest-CoT traces exhibit broader predictive support, more frequent hypothesis switching, greater and more temporally distributed local distributional movement, and later commitment" — efficiency requires different dynamical profile than accuracy.

## Limitations
- L261: "Our analysis is computationally expensive, developing cheaper approximations of answer-distribution trajectories is therefore an important direction for future work" — computational cost limits scalability.
- L261: "Moreover, the comparisons in Section 5.3 are only descriptive, and future controlled studies could better isolate the effects of architecture, scale, and post-training" — study is observational rather than causal.
- L261-262: "Lastly, whether the information that answer-distribution trajectories provide can translate into practical gains remains an open question" — unclear if insights lead to actionable improvements.
- L373-378: "Estimating conditional answer entropy at every token is computationally expensive. We therefore evaluate at checkpoint positions spaced uniformly at stride $s=16$, and always including the final prefix length" — uses checkpointing to reduce computation at cost of granularity.

## Assumptions and conditions
- L61: "We apply a deterministic parser that maps each generated answer sequence $A_{1:T}$ to a single discrete answer label $Y\in\mathcal{A}$" — assumes discrete answer space and deterministic parsing.
- L271-290: "We focus on reasoning tasks with a discrete answer space $\mathcal{A}$. Each example consists of a question $Q\in\mathcal{Q}$ and a ground-truth answer $A\in\mathcal{A}$" — restricts to discrete-answer tasks.
- L358-370: "All continuation rollouts are performed in evaluation mode without gradient computation, and sampling parameters are held fixed across models and prefixes" — uses stochastic sampling without training gradients.
- L289-290: "Invalid or unparsable outputs are mapped to a special null answer category" — handles invalid outputs as a special category.

## Dimensions
- **Evidence**: L57-71 (empirical) — empirical measurement of prefix-conditioned distributions through Monte Carlo rollouts on reasoning traces from trained models.
- **Measures**: L113-134 (monitorability) — defines measurable trajectory-level metrics (exploration, revision, motion, commitment) based on observable predictive distributions over answers.
- **Elicitation**: n/a — paper studies existing reasoning behavior rather than deliberately training or eliciting behaviors.
- **Pressure**: n/a — analysis is observational of deployed reasoning models without applying training pressure.
