- `method name`: confirmed — JIT-Agent (the trained model is JIT-Agent-27B; training stage III is Evo-GDPO; seed codebase HarnessFactory) — "Through this pipeline, we train JIT-Agent-27B, a harness intelligence model that can customize, repair, and evolve task-specific harnesses" (L33)
- `method summary`: corrected — A harness is factored into four modules (memory, planning, action, capability orchestration) under a fixed protocol and a shared execution kernel, with a seed bank of 13 re-implemented scaffolds (ReAct, Plan-and-Execute, ROMA, etc.). A 27B generator is trained in three stages. Stage I is SFT on teacher-generated, validated harnesses, then DPO on Pareto preferences over reward, latency and cost. Stage II imitates teacher repairs that make failed harnesses executable within two rounds. Stage III is Evo-GDPO, a clipped PPO with separately normalised reward, latency and cost advantages against the archive's incumbent. At inference it emits a harness that wraps a frozen off-the-shelf backbone; static mode samples N harnesses and executes one, streaming mode updates the archive across tasks. — "Let $\boldsymbol{\tau}$ be a task, $\pi_{\psi}$ a frozen backbone executor" (L57); "We retain only trajectories that become executable within two rounds" (L137); "JIT-Agent generates $N$ harnesses in parallel, selects one of them, and executes only the selected harness" (L173)
